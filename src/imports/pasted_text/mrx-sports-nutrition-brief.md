Create a premium modern static website for a startup brand called
"MRX Sports & Nutrition".

MRX is a sports and nutrition trading company focused on making
quality nutrition products more accessible to fitness enthusiasts,
athletes and everyday active people.

IMPORTANT BUSINESS MODEL:

MRX does NOT manufacture supplements.

MRX sources established sports nutrition and supplement products
through authorized dealers, distributors and legitimate supply
channels.

The company operates with a lean trading model and sensible margins,
allowing it to offer competitive and accessible pricing.

The website must communicate this transparently and positively.

CORE BRAND PHILOSOPHY:

Premium Nutrition. Fair Prices.

Nutrition for Everyone.

Fitness Today. Longevity Tomorrow.

BRAND VALUES:

AUTHENTICITY
FAIR VALUE
NUTRITION
FITNESS
LONGEVITY
TRANSPARENCY
ACCESSIBILITY

DESIGN STYLE:

Create a premium sports-science aesthetic.

The website should feel like a modern international nutrition brand,
not a cheap supplement store.

Use:
- minimalist layouts
- bold modern typography
- strong visual hierarchy
- premium product photography
- clean grids
- subtle technical/scientific graphics
- sophisticated dark and light sections
- generous whitespace
- modern micro-interactions
- smooth scrolling
- subtle animations
- premium editorial layout

Avoid:
- generic bodybuilding website designs
- excessive flames
- aggressive muscle imagery
- cheap discount-store aesthetics
- excessive gradients
- clutter
- unrealistic supplement claims
